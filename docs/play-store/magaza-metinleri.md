### Full description
```
Turn knowledge into tactics. In Quiz Tactics you answer the same questions at the same moment as your opponents, climb the leagues and aim for a title.

More than 12,000 questions across General Knowledge, Science, History, Literature, Cinema, Sports and more. Choose a Turkish or English interface.

GAME MODES

- Classic — 20 questions, everyone sees the same question at the same time. You pick the jokers you take into the match, and that choice is part of your tactics: 50:50, Second Chance and more.

- Duel — Take control, send your opponent a category and get three answers right in a row. Play tactically with control cards.

- Shared Treasure — The treasure is shared; whoever alone knows the answer decides: OPEN it and take the points, or say KEEP GOING and let it grow.

- Pure Knowledge — No power-ups, just what you know.

- Daily Tournaments — Five sessions every day: 10:00, 14:00, 18:00, 20:00 and 24:00 (Turkey time). Everyone plays the same competition at the same time.

- Group Match and My Mistakes — Play with a group of friends or revisit the questions you got wrong.

LEAGUES AND SEASONS

- Weekly leagues: Bronze, Silver, Gold, Diamond, Legend. In groups of 25, the top 5 move up and the bottom 5 move down; leagues reset every Monday at 00:00 (Turkey time).
- City, country and world rankings.
- Season Path: unlock rewards as you play during 28-day seasons.
- Daily quests, badges and titles.

FRIENDS

- Add friends, invite them with a code, challenge them, ask for a rematch and message them.
- Answer your opponent with four quick in-match reactions.
- Block or report players who bother you.

PERSONALISATION

- Avatars, frames and Answer Signature effects. They are cosmetic only and give no in-game advantage.

SIGN-IN AND ACCOUNT

- Sign in with Google or an email code. You can delete your account inside the app or at quiztactics.com/hesap-sil.

An internet connection is required. Quiz Tactics is for players aged 13 and over.

Web: quiztactics.com
Contact: info@quiztactics.com
Privacy policy: https://quiztactics.com/gizlilik
```

### Full description
```
Quiz Tactics is a real-time trivia game you play against friends or players from all over. Everyone gets the same question at the same moment — the winner is whoever knows, not whoever taps fastest.

GAME MODES

• Classic — A 1v1 trivia match. Everyone who answers correctly in time gets the same points; there is no speed bonus. Use jokers to help you. Play Ranked (league points + full coins) or Casual (no league points).

• Duel — A tactical match. Answer correctly to take control and send your opponent a category. Build a streak of 3 correct answers in a row to win. Question 20 is the joker-free Last Duel.

• Shared Treasure — Two players grow one shared treasure. When only you know the answer, you decide: OPEN it and take the points, or KEEP going and double it. First to 80 points wins.

• Tournaments — Five sessions every day (Turkey time 10:00, 14:00, 18:00, 20:00 and 24:00). Knockout format; ties are settled with a Golden Question.

• Group match, Pure Knowledge and My Mistakes — Play with a group of friends, play a joker-free knowledge match, or review the questions you got wrong.

LEAGUES AND SEASONS

• Weekly leagues: Bronze, Silver, Gold, Diamond, Legend. In groups of 25, the top 5 move up and the bottom 5 move down; leagues reset every Monday.
• City, country and world rankings.
• Season Path: earn Season Points as you play during 28-day seasons and unlock rewards.
• Daily quests, badges and titles.

FRIENDS

• Add friends, invite them with a code, challenge them and ask for a rematch.
• Message your friends.
• Block or report players who bother you.

PERSONALISATION

• Avatars, frames and Answer Signature effects. They are cosmetic only and give no in-game advantage.

IF YOUR CONNECTION DROPS

• In Classic, Pure Knowledge, Duel and Shared Treasure the match waits 60 seconds if you disconnect; when you come back you continue where you left off.

SIGN-IN

• Start playing right away as a guest, or save your account with Google or an email code.
• You can delete your account inside the app or at quiztactics.com/hesap-sil.

An internet connection is required. Quiz Tactics is for players aged 13 and over.

Privacy policy: https://quiztactics.com/gizlilik
```
